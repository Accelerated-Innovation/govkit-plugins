# Epic AI-300 — Client self-service portal

**Elevator Pitch:** Give agency clients a portal where they can see project status, review
deliverables, and answer approval requests themselves, instead of emailing their account
manager for every question.

**User Problems:**

- Clients email account managers for status updates several times a week; answers take
  hours and interrupt delivery work.
- Deliverable approvals happen over email threads; approvals get lost and are hard to audit.
- Clients cannot see which items are blocked waiting on them.

**Success Metrics:**

- 60% reduction in status-request emails to account managers within one quarter of launch
- Median deliverable approval time under 2 business days
- 70% of active clients sign in at least weekly

**Personas:** Client stakeholder (primary), Account manager, Agency admin

**Evidence:** Support inbox audit (March): 41% of inbound client email is a status
question answerable from the project tracker.

**Initial Scope / MVP thinking:** Read-only visibility first; approvals matter most after
that. Client-authored change requests are attractive but not required to launch.

**NFRs noted at epic level:** Client data isolation between accounts is non-negotiable.
Portal must be usable on mobile browsers.
